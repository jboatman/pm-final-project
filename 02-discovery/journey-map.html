StreamLine · Spotlight initiative · Future-state journey

From the 15,000-title scroll to a pick you trust

Persona
The Discerning Curator-Seeker, a film-literate viewer who judges the platform by the quality of its picks.
Goal
Find a hidden gem that fits their taste, from a source they trust, without wading through sequels.
Strategy
Restore brand authority with curation: editors choose the pool, the algorithm matches it to each viewer.

Advantages over the manual workaround

  • No app-switching

    Curation, reasoning, saving and playback happen in one place, not across texts, review sites and search.

  • Personal, not generic

    Spotlight learns from each rating. A competitor's newsletter sends everyone the same two films.

  • Full catalog depth

    Curators surface hidden gems from 15,000 titles, turning the "warehouse" into an advantage.

Win-back entry point: lapsed subscribers like Tom never open the app, so they get a weekly Spotlight email with two explained picks. One tap resubscribes them and drops them into stage 2.
Phase 0 prerequisite: stage 3 only works once watchlist sync (BUG-1058, Critical) and cross-device resume (BUG-1061, High) are fixed. Otherwise Spotlight leads users straight back to "lost it on the TV."
Evidence codes reference the StreamLine sample research set (UXR = interview, BUG = bug report).
