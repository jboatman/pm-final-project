## 1. Context-aware Spotlight ("why now" signals)

**Added:** 2026-11-18 · **Status:** Unvalidated · **Revisit in:** M3 (hypothesis), M5 (experiment)

**Idea:** The matching algorithm weights picks by real-world context: time of day, day of week, weather, season and holidays. A stormy Sunday afternoon might boost slow, immersive or cozy titles; a Friday night might boost lighter crowd-pleasers.

**Why it could differentiate**
- It makes the "why now" in "why this, why now" literal: *"It's raining and it's Sunday. Here's a slow-burn mystery for the afternoon."*
- It answers mood-based asks in the research: Beth (UXR-09) wants "quiet Sunday" picks and a way to browse by mood.
- None of the curation leaders (Criterion, MUBI, Kanopy) personalizes by context; their programming is the same for everyone.

**What we'd need to validate**
- Does context actually change what people choose? Compare viewing data by weather, time and day for a clear signal.
- Do viewers find it helpful or creepy? Test the explanation copy in interviews.
- Does it lift Spotlight play-through vs. picks without context?

**Risks and open questions**
- **Privacy:** weather needs location. Use coarse location (city level) or the account's region, and make it optional.
- **Gimmick risk:** if the context link feels forced, it weakens trust in the editor's note instead of strengthening it.
- **Priority:** this is an enhancement to Spotlight's matching, not part of the core bet. Keep it out of the MVP unless testing shows strong lift.

**Possible experiment (M5):** A/B test Spotlight picks with and without context weighting; measure play-through rate and "nothing watched" sessions.
