## 1. Chosen scenario

**Path:** StreamLine Spotlight (B2C)

A big catalog with flat engagement is a clear, relatable discovery problem, where curation can win back quality-focused viewers.

## 2. The strategic crisis

_The big-picture business risk. If the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?_

> If we do nothing for 12 months, the company will be locked into the "warehouse of mid-tier content" label. Engagement will slide from a plateau into decline as quality-focused viewers move to curation-led rivals, leaving us competing on catalog size and price, a race we lose to bigger libraries.

## 3. The moment of misery

_The specific point where the product fails the user, forcing a manual workaround (Google, spreadsheets, group texts, a competitor)._

> The user is currently forced to leave the app to decide what to watch: Googling "best movies on StreamLine," scrolling Reddit or Rotten Tomatoes, or texting friends "what's good?" After 15+ minutes of look-alike tiles, many give up, rewatch something familiar, or open a competitor.

## 4. Problem hook

_One urgent sentence fusing the business risk and the user pain, your pitch for why this is the most important thing to work on right now._

> We must stop StreamLine's slide into a commoditized, mid-tier content warehouse by fixing the endless, low-trust browsing that pushes quality-seeking viewers to Google, Reddit and competitors just to find something worth watching.

## 5. Value proposition

_Who it's for, the new value, and why it's urgent to launch now._

> For **discerning viewers who value quality over volume**, we will **replace the 15,000-title scroll with Spotlight: a small, trusted, curated set of standout picks matched to their taste and explained ("why this, why now")** because **every month we wait, high-value segments churn to curation-led competitors and the "warehouse" perception gets harder to reverse.**

## 6. Cold-read self-review

_Read your hook back as a skeptical stakeholder. If you don't feel the urgency, that's your data._

| Question | Your answer |
|---|---|
| Is the business risk high-stakes enough to justify a new initiative? | High-stakes: a plateau in a subscription business leads to churn and pricing pressure. Needs one engagement or churn data point to prove it. |
| Is the moment of misery systemic, or just an edge case? | Systemic: it hits the core action (choosing what to watch) every session, and a bigger catalog makes it worse. Workarounds still need confirming in discovery. |
| Does the value proposition actually remove the obstacle? | Yes, if Spotlight replaces the scroll with fewer, explained picks. If it's just another "Top 10" carousel, it's a generic feature. |
